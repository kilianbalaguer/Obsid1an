Description

ShardFetch is a system information tool for jailbroken iOS devices, inspired by neofetch and fastfetch. Run shardfetch in your terminal to see your device info alongside a rainbow Apple logo.


Displays: iOS version, device model, jailbreak type, uptime, CPU, memory, storage, battery, shell, and terminal.

Compatibility

  • iOS 7.0 — 27.0.1 (arm64)
  • Rootful, Rootless, and Roothide
  • Auto-detects jailbreak environment
